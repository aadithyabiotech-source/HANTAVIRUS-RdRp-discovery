# Future Work

## Molecular Dynamics Simulations

Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations were not performed in the present study.
They are planned as future work to evaluate the stability of the predicted
protein–ligand complexes.

## Experimental Validation

Experimental validation is required to confirm the predicted antiviral activity
of the prioritized candidates.

## Planned Studies

- Molecular dynamics simulations for Gen2_Trifluoromethyl and selected scaffold-hopped candidates.
- Binding free-energy calculations.
- In vitro antiviral assays against Hantaan virus.
- Cytotoxicity and selectivity testing.
- Optimization of the most promising scaffold-hopped candidates.
